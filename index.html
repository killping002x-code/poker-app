How to use

  1. Tap the green microphone and allow it if Chrome asks.
  2. Speak. Pause and it sends itself, or tap the microphone again.
  3. Check the Hand tab to see what the app understood. Fix anything there by hand.

Things you can say

  • "Cash game, stack 100 big blinds"
  • "Tournament, stack 25"
  • "I have ace king suited on the button"
  • "Pocket queens in the big blind"
  • "Nine eight of hearts" (suits: spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs)
  • "Villain is in the cutoff"
  • "Flop is king seven two"
  • "Turn jack of clubs" / "River four"
  • "Pot is 12" / "Facing a bet of 8"
  • "I call" / "I raise to 30" (goes in the action log)

Commands

"new hand", "clear my cards", "clear board", "save hand", "summary", "repeat that", "stop talking".

Notes

Say numbers as digits (12, not twelve). Amounts are in big blinds. Enter the pot as it is now, including the bet you face. If you do not say a suit, the card shows with a dashed border and no suit.

Pot odds shown are plain math: the equity needed to break even on a call.

Microphone problems

Open the page in Chrome. If blocked: Chrome menu → Settings → Site settings → Microphone. Voice recognition needs internet. You can always type, or use the keyboard microphone key.

Learning tool only

This app is for practice, learning and reviewing hands you have already played. Do not use it for live decisions in real-money games where outside assistance is not allowed.
